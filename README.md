# 🚀 StudyPilot — AI-Powered Personalized Study Planning & Adaptive Learning Platform

StudyPilot is an AI-powered learning platform designed to help students plan their preparation, practice through AI-generated quizzes, identify weak topics, and adapt their study strategy based on performance.

The platform combines AI-powered study planning, quiz evaluation, weak-topic detection, adaptive learning, progress tracking, and AI-generated revision notes into one application.

# ✨ Features

## 📚 AI-Powered Study Plan

Generate a personalized study plan based on:

- Syllabus
- Exam date
- Daily available study hours

The AI creates a structured preparation plan to help students organize their study schedule effectively.

---

## 🧠 AI-Powered Quizzes

Generate topic-specific multiple-choice quizzes using AI.

### Features include:

- 5 AI-generated questions
- 4 options per question
- Instant evaluation
- Score calculation
- Correct answer review
- AI-generated explanations

Students can use quizzes to test their understanding and identify areas that require more practice.

---

## 📊 Quiz History

Track previous quiz attempts and performance.

Students can view:

- Quiz topic
- Score
- Percentage
- Attempt date and time

Quiz history also provides the performance data used for weak-topic detection and adaptive learning.

---

## 🎯 Weak Topic Detection

StudyPilot analyzes quiz performance and automatically identifies weak topics.

Topics with lower performance are highlighted so students know where they need more practice and revision.

This helps students focus their preparation on the areas where they need the most improvement.

---

## 🔄 Adaptive Study Plan

The study plan can adapt according to quiz performance.

The system analyzes:

- Quiz scores
- Weak topics
- Remaining preparation time

Based on this information, StudyPilot generates an updated study plan focused on areas that need improvement.
